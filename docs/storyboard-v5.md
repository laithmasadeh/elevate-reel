# Gym reel v5: visuals rethought from his script (19 Nov 2026)
Supersedes the visual table in gym-reel-v4. Script unchanged: claude/gym-reel-v4-counter-split-screen-19-nov.md.

## What was wrong with v4
- The split was a small tablet on the counter, not the screen. He asked for the screen split in three, one panel per customer and per discount.
- Customers were reduced to cards. His script is about three PEOPLE in three situations.
- Visuals showed the lesson (check marks, turnstile) instead of the story beat he wrote.

## Concept: one counter, three bands, same moment
The whole 9:16 frame is split into three stacked bands (each 1080x640, thin teal divider). Each band is the SAME gym counter, same camera, same light, one customer's hands and props at the counter. No faces, no pawns. Style: glossy 3D product render, Elevate teal studio (#175553 / #1D847D / #0F3634 with tints #D6E9E8 / #EFF6F6), white for text and cards. Gold is a small highlight only (a thin edge or a small star on the active offer tag, never a filled tag, prop, card or background). Other colours (soft neutrals, a muted warm tone for contrast) only where they look better. One style on every frame.
Customers by props only:
- Member: teal wristband and membership card
- Competitor's customer: a gym bag with a generic invented logo (no real brand) and a rival card
- Price-sensitive: wallet open, hand holding a price card, hesitating
